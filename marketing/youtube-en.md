### Full Description

**CC Suite - The Standard Library for Claude Code**

Tired of AI hallucinations? Frustrated by "jagged intelligence" where your AI assistant is brilliant one moment and completely wrong the next?

CC Suite solves this with three powerful tools:

**CrossCheck - Multi-Model Verification**
Use AI to verify AI. Claude, Codex, and Gemini cross-check each other in 3 rounds of adversarial verification. Only consensus answers get through. Result: 90%+ reduction in hallucinations.

**SocialPublisher - Trend Discovery to Distribution**
Find what's trending in tech. Let AI extract the core insights. Publish to Twitter, WeChat, and Xiaohongshu with one click. Amplify your tech influence.

**BorisWorkflow - Best Practices Packaged**
Boris Cherny, the creator of Claude Code at Anthropic, shares best practices on Twitter. We packaged them into one-line commands. /init, /setup-permissions, /create-subagent, /ralph-loop - all the wisdom, none of the setup.

**The Ultimate Workflow Loop:**
Discover → Verify → Develop → Review → Publish

From trending topics to shipped features. From ideas to impact.
